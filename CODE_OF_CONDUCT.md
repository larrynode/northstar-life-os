# Code of Conduct

We want Northstar Life OS to be a welcoming, low-noise project for people who
care about intentional work and local data ownership. Contributors,
maintainers, and community members are expected to:

- be respectful of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- discuss code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than people;
- accept good-faith corrections and credit the work of others;
- make room for quieter participants; and
- protect privacy by removing personal records, credentials, and private logs
  from public discussions.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized or violent language,
doxxing, spam, deliberate misinformation, and attempts to obtain credentials
or private life data are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close material that violates this code and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as possible. Use the repository's private security advisory
channel for sensitive reports; do not open a public issue containing private
details.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
